# T-34: Treatises

- **Risk:** low.
- **Reads:** SPEC.md section 4, row T-34; criterion AC-68; docs/GAME-DESIGN.md section 4.7 (the Treatises
  paragraph); src/core/research.ts, src/core/cost.ts, src/core/jobs.ts, src/core/data.ts.
- **May change:** src/core/state.ts, src/core/research.ts, src/core/jobs.ts, src/core/actions.ts, src/core/data.ts,
  test/research.test.ts.
- **Build:** once every research is bought, murmurs buy Treatises: each makes every job 5% better, compounding.
- **How:**
  - `state.treatises` starts at 0. In data.ts: `TREATISE_BASE = 20000` and, written exactly,
    `export const TREATISE_FACTOR = 1.5;`.
  - `buyTreatise(state)`: refused unless every item of `RESEARCH` is bought; costs
    `costOf(TREATISE_BASE, TREATISE_FACTOR, state.treatises)` murmurs; refused with too few murmurs.
  - Every job's output is multiplied by `1.05 ** state.treatises`.
  - Treatises reset at a bake (T-15 does the reset; nothing to do here).
  - Required examples, each an assertion: with one research missing, a treatise is refused even with 1,000,000
    murmurs; the first costs 20,000, the second 30,000, the third 45,000; with 2 treatises a Miller makes
    0.4 × 1.1025 flour a second.
- **Done means:** `npx vitest run -t "research: Treatises open after every research"`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, the gates.
