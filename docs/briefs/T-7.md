# T-7: buildings

- **Risk:** low.
- **Reads:** SPEC.md section 4, row T-7; criteria AC-17, AC-59; docs/GAME-DESIGN.md sections 3.4 and 4.5;
  src/core/cost.ts, src/core/state.ts, src/core/data.ts.
- **May change:** src/core/buildings.ts, src/core/actions.ts, src/core/state.ts, src/core/step.ts,
  src/core/data.ts, test/buildings.test.ts.
- **Build:** the eight buildings of the design's table: buying one, buying as many as can be afforded, and
  their effects.
- **How:**
  - `BUILDINGS` in data.ts: for each, its needed research, its cost as a list of `{ resource, base }`, its
    factor and its effect, exactly as the design's table. The Crumb Cathedral costs both rise and tang.
  - `buy(state, name)` and `buyMax(state, name)` use cost.ts. A building with two costs is affordable only if
    both are. A refused buy changes nothing.
  - Effects: housing (Bubble +5, Gluten Hall +25, Crumb Cathedral +120), `flourCap` (Pantry +500), Bran Mill
    (Millers +25% each, added), Archive (Ponderers +20% each, added), Warm Spot (every job ×1.1 each,
    compounding), Acid Bastion (guard HP +10% each, added; store it, the Fridge uses it in T-11).
  - **AC-59:** a test walks every entry of `BUILDINGS` (and of the research table once T-8 adds it) and fails if
    any cost names flour or sugar.
- **Done means:** `npx vitest run -t "buildings: costs grow by their factor"`,
  `npx vitest run -t "costs: nothing costs flour or sugar"`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, `bash tests/gates.sh`.
