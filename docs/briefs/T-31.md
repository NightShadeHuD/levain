# T-31: hydration and discard

- **Risk:** low.
- **Reads:** SPEC.md section 4, row T-31; criteria AC-60, AC-61; docs/GAME-DESIGN.md sections 4.4 and 4.6;
  src/core/state.ts, src/core/growth.ts, src/core/jobs.ts, src/core/actions.ts, src/core/research.ts, src/core/data.ts.
- **May change:** src/core/state.ts, src/core/growth.ts, src/core/jobs.ts, src/core/actions.ts, src/core/data.ts,
  test/hydration.test.ts, test/discard.test.ts.
- **Build:** the hydration slider and the Discard action.
- **How:**
  - `state.hydration` starts at 1. `setHydration(state, h)` is refused (state unchanged) unless the research
    "Hydration" is bought; otherwise it keeps h between 0.5 and 1.5 and rounds it to the nearest 0.1, stored
    as `Math.round(h * 10) / 10` (so 0.73 becomes 0.7 and 2 becomes 1.5).
  - Growth is multiplied by `state.hydration`, Rise by `state.hydration`, and Tang by the value written exactly
    `const tangMult = 2 - state.hydration;` (once, in jobs.ts).
  - `discard(state, percent)`: refused unless "Fermentation" is bought and percent is 10, 25 or 50. It removes
    `Math.floor(idle * percent / 100)` idle cells (that text exactly, once) and adds one cracker for each. Idle
    is counted as T-6 counts it. Jobs are never touched.
  - Required examples, each an assertion: at hydration 0.5 Tang is ×1.5 and Rise ×0.5; at 1.5 Tang ×0.5 and Rise
    ×1.5; at 1 both ×1; `setHydration(state, 0.73)` stores 0.7; `setHydration(state, 2)` stores 1.5; 37 idle cells
    discarded at 25% gives 9 crackers and leaves 28 idle; 3 idle at 10% gives 0 crackers and changes nothing else;
    a discard of 30% is refused.
- **Done means:** `npx vitest run -t "hydration: growth, rise and tang follow the slider"`,
  `npx vitest run -t "discard: idle cells become crackers"`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, the gates.
- **Breaks:**

```
hydration: growth, rise and tang follow the slider fails when src/core/jobs.ts has `const tangMult = 2 - state.hydration;` as `const tangMult = state.hydration;`
discard: idle cells become crackers fails when src/core/actions.ts has `Math.floor(idle * percent / 100)` as `Math.ceil(idle * percent / 100)`
```
